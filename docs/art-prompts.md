## Generator Tips

### Negative Prompt (add to all)
> blurry, realistic photo, 3D render, isometric, side view, text, watermark, signature, low quality, deformed

### Platform-Specific Tips
- **Midjourney:** Add `--style raw --ar 1:1 --s 250` at the end
- **DALL-E:** Use as-is, specify "pixel art" clearly
- **Stable Diffusion:** Use the negative prompt, add `pixel art, sprite sheet` to positive
